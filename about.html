Our Story

No chaff. Just kuku.

How one charcoal stall in Jomvu Jitoni turned into the plaza everyone routes their evening through.

Nochaf Kuku Plaza started as a single charcoal stall in Jomvu Jitoni and grew into the corner everyone on the Mombasa side routes their evening through. Nochaf means what it says — no filler, no chaff, just kuku done right: marinated overnight, grilled low and slow, and handed to you still smoking.

Every bird is grilled to order. Every plate goes out with kachumbari, ugali or chapati, and a story from whoever's manning the grill that day. We're not chasing trends — we're chasing the smell of charcoal at 6pm and the sound of a plaza full of people who came back for seconds.

Whether you're stopping by after work, feeding the whole family, or just craving something grilled properly, Nochaf Kuku Plaza is built for one thing: good kuku, no shortcuts.
